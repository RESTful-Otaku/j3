# Official & moderator guide

Officials score only contests they are assigned to; runners/admins may override any contest.

## Get assigned

Open `/official`. The feed lists your assigned bouts (runners see all). Pair your judge phone via
the mat assignment shown in the control room.

## Select the contest and corner

Choose the active contest and the red/blue corner before scoring. Corner colours and glyphs are
configured by the organiser and never depend on colour alone.

## Log a technique

Search the 400-entry catalogue by English, romaji, Japanese script, style or movement tag. Pick the
configured point value; the server resolves points/kind/card/label from the rule snapshot, so the
client cannot spoof them. Recent moves appear on the scoreboard.

## Penalties and cards

Award yellow/red cards or shido from the configured ladder. Cards show on the correct fighter's
side; DQ thresholds follow the event rules.

## Control the clock

Start, pause and resume the authoritative mat timer. Pausing on referee stoppage flashes the board;
resume only on the scorekeeper's command. Hold/pin timers count down live.

## Technical wins and losses

Record injury, forfeit, no-show, withdrawal, referee, cheating or DQ using the fight outcome
vocabulary. The bout locks to `forfeit` with an audited technical event and SSE fan-out.

## Submissions and instant wins

Tap/verbal submission ends the bout immediately and overrides accumulated points; promotion follows
the configured rules.

## Kata panel judging

Panel phones submit scores/deductions; the server drops high/low and computes the authoritative
total. Shitei/tokuyi rounds, bunkai and advancement are tracked separately.

## Finish the bout

Confirm Red/Blue/Draw. Confirmation asks again for major outcomes; results lock and celebrations
fire. Hand over any incident notes to the runner.
