# Formats, pools & promotion

## Selecting a format

Formats follow the event's `FormatPolicy` and actual entrant count. J3 suggests round robin when
elimination is impossible, and records the chosen format per stage.

## Round robin

Circle-method scheduling gives every fighter the same number of bouts. Standings use the configured
ordered tie-break chain, and every pairing appears once.

## Pools

Pool generation balances size and skill, seeds by rank/stats and spreads same-club fighters where
allowed. Organisers can drag fighters between pools and regenerate with a new random seed.

## Elimination brackets

Single and double elimination are both supported, with explicit true-double vs consolation-double
semantics. BYEs are allocated by seed/title; first-round club/country separation is configurable.

## Third place and bronze

If the two losing semi-finalists have not met, a third-place rematch is scheduled. If they have, two
bronze medals are awarded. This is a per-discipline rule.

## Promotion and history

Winners/losers/draws advance according to rule config. Each round keeps a copy of the fighter card,
so brackets show the full path. Repechage, forfeits and no-shows rebalance affected slots.
