# Participant & spectator guide

## Create your profile

Self-register as fighter or spectator. Add photo, contact details, DOB, height/weight (metric or
imperial; stored metric), belt/rank, club affiliation or independent status. Minors need guardian
consent. Settings hold language, units, appearance and profile data.

## Register for the right division

J3 offers only categories compatible with your stats. Full or incompatible categories are greyed
out. A heavier/older division may be offered with explicit consent to move up.

## Before the day

Read the public event details: rules, required kit/protection, times, venue and schedule. Check your
schedule and notifications for callouts.

## At the venue

Check in and weigh in at the desk; QR badges identify you. Watch `/public` for "you're up next" and
follow your mat/bracket. On phones you only see public, participant and club screens — operator
tools stay on larger displays.

## Results and appeals

Results appear on tables and brackets with W/L/D glyphs and outcome codes. Ask your club leader or
the runner about corrections; every change is audited.

## Spectators

Spectators see only published data: dashboard, schedule, brackets, tables, pools and public details.
No scoring drafts, private notes or personal data are exposed.
