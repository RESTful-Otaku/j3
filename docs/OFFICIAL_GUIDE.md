# Official & moderator guide

Your console is designed for one tap per action and confirmation before anything is committed. Use **Guides & progress → Officials** for a checked, hands-on run-through.

## Get assigned

A runner assigns officials to mats and contests. Sign in with your official account and open **Official console**. If you cannot see a contest, you are not assigned to it — ask the runner rather than trying to score from another account.

## Select the contest and corner

1. Choose the contest in **Select contest**.
2. Tap **RED** or **BLUE** to choose the active corner. The button shows the fighter's name.
3. Search the technique palette by English name or romaji.

Points, penalties and submissions always apply to the active corner. Check the name before every entry.

## Log a technique

Tap a technique card, review the confirmation dialogue (technique, romaji and point value), then press **Confirm & log**. The score appears on the live scoreboard immediately. If you tapped the wrong corner or technique, use **Undo last entry** — the correction is audited.

## Penalties and cards

Penalty cards are in the same palette. A yellow card records a light foul; repeated fouls escalate according to the event's rule set. Red cards and disqualifications follow the configured penalty ladder. Never award a card without the confirmation step.

## Control the clock

The scorekeeper's **Mat control** is authoritative. Start the bout, pause for referee stoppages and resume on instruction. The public board mirrors this clock. Do not run a separate stopwatch as the source of truth.

## Submissions and instant wins

A submission overrides accumulated points. Use **Red submits** or **Blue submits** for an instant win; J3 records the tap and asks you to confirm the final result. See [scoring rules](/docs/scoring) for holds, golden score and tie-breaks.

## Kata panel judging

Switch to the **Kata panel** tab. Each performance has sections such as etiquette, demonstration and application, scored by each judge from 0–10. J3 drops the highest and lowest marks where the rule set requires it and publishes the authoritative total. Change a score and confirm; totals update on **Tables & rankings**.

## Finish the bout

Use **Red wins**, **Blue wins** or **Draw** in the Finish & override panel, then confirm. The result is locked, pushed to every display and written to the audit trail. If you need to correct it, ask a runner to use the recorded undo path.

## Handover

At the end of your assignment, check **Audit trail** for your actions and tell the runner about any incidents. Do not share your account with another official — assignments and audit entries must stay attributable.
