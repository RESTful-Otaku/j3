# Participant & spectator guide

Whether you are fighting, coaching or watching, J3 gives you a public view that never exposes private scoring drafts. Use **Guides & progress → Participants** for a checked tour of the main views.

## Create your profile

On **Settings**, add your name, contact details, date of birth, height, weight, rank/grade and club. Enter metric or imperial units — the server converts and stores metric values as canonical. Belt colour is chosen from a colour block with a custom option for tenant-specific grades.

## Register for the right division

Open the public sign-up page. J3 only offers categories you are compatible with based on age, weight, height and grade. Heavier or older divisions appear only if you explicitly consent to move up. Full categories are greyed out so you do not waste time applying.

Minors need guardian consent before registration is accepted.

## Before the day

Read **Event config** for required kit, match length, venue, schedule, amenities and rest rules. Check the **Public board** for your next-up callout. Arrive in time for weigh-in; officials record your official weight and flag any out-of-class issue.

## At the venue

- **Public board** — live contests, venue clock and "next up".
- **Fight pools** — your group and scheduled opponents.
- **Brackets** — progression through each round, including bronze decisions.
- **Live scoreboard** — your contest with belt, club logo, moves and penalties.
- **Tables & rankings** — standings, club points and medals.

If you are called to a mat, the announcer's toast flashes your card. Follow the mat number shown on the board.

## Results and appeals

Once a bout is confirmed, the result is locked. W/L/D always show a letter as well as a colour. If you believe a result is wrong, speak to the runner — corrections are made through a recorded audit path, not by editing the board directly.

## Spectators

You do not need an account to watch public views. Open `/public`, `/brackets`, `/tables` and `/pools` in separate tabs or screens. Every view is URL-addressable and updates live.
